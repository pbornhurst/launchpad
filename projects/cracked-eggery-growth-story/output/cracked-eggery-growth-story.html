Methodology

  • Matched window: equal-length pre vs post for each store (79d for Cleveland Park, 58d for 8th St). Cleanest apples-to-apples delta.
  • Full pre / weekly run-rate: 6mo before install vs post-to-date, normalized to per-week so window-length differences don't distort.
  • POS GOV is by definition 100% incremental - these stores had no in-store/kiosk channel reporting to DD before Pathfinder. Pre-install in-store/kiosk = $0 in edw.merchant.fact_merchant_orders_portal.
  • Marketplace ops (errors, rating, wait, cancellations) computed on the matched window only - these are completed-order-level metrics where seasonality matters less.
  • Sources: edw.merchant.fact_merchant_orders_portal (channels, ops, cx), edw.ads.fact_sl_campaign_performance (SL), edw.ads.fact_promo_campaign_performance (promos).
