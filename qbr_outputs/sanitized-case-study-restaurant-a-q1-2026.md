# Pathfinder Customer Case Study — Q1 2026 Growth
**Sanitized for Sales Use** | Period: Feb 1 – Apr 30, 2026 vs. Nov 1, 2025 – Jan 31, 2026 | Restaurant A

## Slide 1 — Headline: 28% GOV Growth in 3 Months on Pathfinder

A single-location, American QSR with a burger-led menu grew the business meaningfully across every channel in the first three months after stabilizing on Pathfinder POS. The merchant ran no merchant-funded promotions during the period — this growth came from ops + a paid-channel media spend lift.

| Hero Metric | Prior 3M | Current 3M | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total GOV (all channels) | $64,098 | $94,375 | **+47.2%** |
| Pathfinder POS GOV | $54,565 | $69,926 | **+28.2%** |
| Total Orders (all channels) | 2,464 | 3,512 | **+42.5%** |
| New Customers (online) | 148 | 376 | **+154.1%** |
| Sponsored Listing ROAS | 4.08x | 5.11x | **+1.03x** |

**Why sales reps should care:** the merchant 4×'d their online customer pipeline while keeping in-store ops clean. That is the Pathfinder value prop in one slide.

## Slide 3 — Channel Mix & Pathfinder Impact

In-store revenue (the Pathfinder-attributable channel) grew 25% on its own, but the bigger story is **how Pathfinder unlocked omnichannel growth**. With the POS stable, the merchant scaled Marketplace and launched Storefront — and the in-store base held while online doubled.

| Channel | Prior 3M Sales | Current 3M Sales | Δ Sales | Prior Orders | Current Orders | Δ Orders |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **In-store (Pathfinder)** | $53,305 | $66,534 | **+24.8%** | 2,101 | 2,622 | +24.8% |
| **Kiosk (Pathfinder)** | $303 | $1,486 | **+390.0%** | 15 | 69 | +360.0% |
| **Marketplace** | $10,331 | $20,816 | **+101.5%** | 344 | 659 | +91.6% |
| **Storefront (1P)** | $150 | $5,539 | **+3,601%** | 4 | 162 | +3,950% |
| **Total** | $64,099 | $94,375 | **+47.2%** | 2,464 | 3,512 | +42.5% |

**Pathfinder direct contribution:** In-store + Kiosk grew from $53.6K to $68.0K (+27%) — that's the volume the merchant could not have processed without Pathfinder POS. Storefront effectively launched in the current period (4 → 162 orders), made possible by the POS-integrated 1P online ordering.
